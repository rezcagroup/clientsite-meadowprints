Ordering & pricing

Many of our products have no minimum - order a single embroidered polo or a handful of tees. Screen printing is most cost-effective at 12+ pieces, but we'll always show you the best method for your quantity.
Your price depends on the garment, the number of ink colors or stitches, and quantity. The more you order, the lower your per-item price. Every quote includes free art setup - no hidden fees.

Embroidery

Printing applies ink to the fabric - great for bold, colorful, or detailed graphics. Embroidery stitches your design with thread for a raised, premium finish that's perfect for logos on polos, caps, and jackets.
No - logo digitizing (converting your art into a stitch file) is free with your embroidery order. We keep the file on hand for fast, consistent reorders.
Left-chest logos are typically up to about 4 inches, while jacket backs can go much larger. We'll recommend the ideal size for your garment and design during proofing.

Turnaround & shipping

Standard production is about two weeks after you approve your proof, plus shipping. Need it sooner? Rush production can turn orders around in as few as 3 business days.
Yes. When you give us a hard deadline, we guarantee your order arrives on time - or we make it right. Just tell us your date on your quote.
Shipping is free on orders over $100. For smaller orders, we charge affordable flat-rate shipping shown before you check out. We can also split-ship to multiple addresses.

Guarantee & returns

If your order isn't right - a misprint, a defect, or it doesn't match your approved proof - we'll reprint it or refund it. Your satisfaction is 100% guaranteed.
You can change or cancel anytime before you approve your proof at no charge. Once production begins we may not be able to make changes, so review your proof carefully.
