How ordering works

No design degree, no order minimums on most items, and a real human in your corner from first sketch to final stitch.

Start an OrderTalk to a Specialist

1. Request it

Pick a product, tell us your quantity, and upload your artwork or logo. Prefer help? Send us your idea and we'll mock it up at no cost.

2. Approve a proof

Within 24 hours you'll get a digital proof showing exact colors, placement, and sizing. Nothing prints until you say yes.

3. We produce it

Your order is screen printed, DTG printed, or embroidered in-house by people who care about every detail.

4. It ships to you

Carefully packed and tracked to your door - free on orders over $100, with rush options when the clock is ticking.

Decoration methods

The right technique for your art

We're method-agnostic. We recommend what makes your design look best and fits your budget - never the other way around.

  • Screen printing - the value champion for 12+ pieces and bold, long-lasting graphics.
  • Direct-to-garment (DTG) - photo-detail, unlimited colors, and no minimums.
  • Embroidery - a premium, textured finish for polos, hats, and jackets.
  • Heat transfer & names/numbers - perfect for personalized team rosters.
