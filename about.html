Our story

Custom gear, made by people who care

Meadow Prints & Embroidery is an independent shop built on a simple belief: every order - whether it's six shirts or six hundred - deserves real craftsmanship and a real human behind it.

It started in a garage with one press

We opened Meadow Prints because we were tired of custom apparel that felt like a factory transaction - impersonal service, mystery pricing, and shirts that cracked after a wash. So we built the opposite.

Today we run screen printing, direct-to-garment, and embroidery under one roof, but the promise hasn't changed: honest prices, a free proof every time, and the kind of care you'd expect from a neighbor, not a call center.

We're proud to help teams, schools, small businesses, and families across the country show up as their best, most unified selves.
